# Sky Star Spa – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Sky Star Spa is a massage and wellness establishment located in Deira, Dubai, offering a range of massage treatments and Moroccan bath experiences.

The website targets residents, visitors and individuals seeking relaxation, massage therapy and traditional spa treatments in Dubai.

The redesigned homepage focuses on establishing a stronger spa identity, presenting treatments more clearly and making contact and location information easier to access. The overall direction emphasises a welcoming, relaxing and professional wellness experience.

## 2. Three Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Positioning
- Introduces clearer messaging focused on massage, relaxation and spa experiences.
- Gives greater prominence to Sky Star Spa's identity and Deira location.
- Creates a more engaging introduction that communicates the services customers can expect.

**Problem addressed:** The previous homepage lacked a distinctive spa identity and did not immediately communicate the business's offering or location.

### 2. Clearer and More Consistent Treatment Descriptions
- Improves the presentation of individual massage treatments.
- Organises the service information to make treatments easier to understand and compare.
- Focuses descriptions on the actual services offered by Sky Star Spa, including Moroccan bath experiences.

**Problem addressed:** The previous treatment descriptions were inconsistent and sometimes relied on generic information rather than clearly explaining the spa's own services.

### 3. Better Contact and Location Visibility
- Gives telephone and WhatsApp contact options greater prominence.
- Improves the visibility of the business address and opening hours.
- Makes essential contact information easier to find throughout the homepage.
- Prioritises convenient access to contact details on mobile devices.

**Problem addressed:** Important business information was available but lacked sufficient prominence, making it harder for visitors to contact the spa or locate it quickly.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Establishes a more distinctive spa-focused visual identity that communicates relaxation and wellness.
- **Layout:** Creates a clearer hierarchy between the introduction, massage treatments, Moroccan bath services and contact information.
- **User Experience:** Makes treatment discovery and service comparison more straightforward.
- **Content Consistency:** Uses clearer, more consistent descriptions across the treatment offering.
- **Conversion Focus:** Improves access to telephone, WhatsApp and location information.
- **Overall Appearance:** Creates a more polished and welcoming homepage aligned with Sky Star Spa's massage and wellness positioning.
